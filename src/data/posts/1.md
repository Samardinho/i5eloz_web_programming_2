## Champions League Final 2025: Key Moments

The 2025 UEFA Champions League final was a spectacle, with Manchester City facing Real Madrid at Wembley Stadium. The match was a rollercoaster of emotions, with both teams showcasing their attacking prowess.

### The First Half: A Tactical Battle

The first half saw both teams playing cautiously, with Manchester City dominating possession at 62%, while Real Madrid relied on counter-attacks. A brilliant save by Real Madrid's goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois kept the score at 0-0.

### The Second Half: Drama Unfolds

In the 67th minute, Vinicius Jr. scored a stunning solo goal for Real Madrid, dribbling past three defenders. Manchester City equalized in the 82nd minute with a header from Kevin De Bruyne. The match seemed destined for extra time until Erling Haaland struck in the 89th minute, sealing a 2-1 victory for City with a powerful shot from outside the box.

### Key Takeaways

*   Erling Haaland proved why he's one of the best strikers in the world.
*   Real Madrid's defense struggled to contain City's relentless attacks.
*   The match ended with a dramatic penalty save by Ederson in stoppage time.
